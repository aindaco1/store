Join **The Perfect Casino Night**, a one-night film mixer with card games, film screenings, hors d’oeuvres, beverages, and a ticketed raffle. All proceeds support *How To Run A Perfect Casino*, a 35mm short film written and directed by **Jack McLaughlin**.

**Saturday, November 7, 2026 · 7–10 p.m.** **Black-tie dress code.**

## Ticket tiers

### Player — $27

- Full access to the event and film screenings.
- 100 chips for the card games and raffle.
- Complimentary hors d’oeuvres and beverages.

### Pit-Boss — $45

Everything in Player, plus:

- 150 extra chips for the card games and raffle (250 total).
- Entry into the “Best Dressed” contest and “Lucky Shot Raffle.”
- A social media shout-out and photo op.

### Executive — $70

Everything in Pit-Boss, plus:

- Specially assigned seating.
- A plated meal served at your table.
- Your name in a special “Thank You” section of the film.

## Executive menu

Your meal includes Greek salad, pan-roasted vegetables, and a red velvet thimble cake, with your choice of entrée:

- Orange-glazed grilled salmon.
- Asiago chicken in a roasted red pepper sauce.
- Plant-based shepherd’s pie.

**Executive meal choices will be arranged after purchase.**
