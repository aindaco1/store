Date: 2026-08-27

- Scope: settled tip-revenue reporting and one-row Orders export actions.
- Previous independently reversible release: `v1.3.3`.
- Shared dependency: Dust Wave Platform `v0.34.1` at
  `ae380c43a16af352ae946f47dd1b7aa4e5b093f0`.

## Behavior and administration contract

- The Analytics summary reports `tipAmountCents` only from orders that satisfy
  the existing settled-order predicate. Payment-pending and failed checkout
  attempts do not contribute tip revenue.
- Unfiltered Tip Revenue is the exact sum of persisted settled-order tips. When
  Analytics is scoped to one product, each mixed-order tip is allocated in
  proportion to the selected product's persisted line-item subtotal; the full
  order tip is not credited to every product.
- Orders CSV and attendee export actions share one responsive action row on
  desktop and mobile. The existing exports, filters, and authorization boundary
  are unchanged.
- Analytics remains an authenticated, private/no-store admin read. The release
  adds no customer tracking, profiling, persistence, mutation, or export field.

## Ethical review

- The metric aggregates an amount already persisted in canonical order totals
  and exposes it only inside the existing authenticated Analytics surface.
- Settlement filtering prevents abandoned or failed checkout tips from being
  represented as money made. Proportional product attribution avoids duplicating
  a mixed order's full tip across multiple product views.
- The layout change preserves both export actions and their existing access
  controls while making their visual relationship clearer.
- No new personal data, behavioral tracking, customer messaging, automated
  decision, or provider mutation is introduced.
