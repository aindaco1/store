## Behavior and audience contract

- New ticket and RSVP drafts enable the post-event email by default. Publishing
  an enable transition records `event_details.followup.enabled_at`.
- At event end plus the configured delay, cron reconciles all confirmed
  first-party purchasers for the event, including orders confirmed before the
  feature was enabled, and deduplicates them by normalized email address.
- Activation must occur before the scheduled send time. At or after the cutoff,
  the product editor disables the field and the Worker rejects attempts to
  change it. Legacy enabled records without a valid activation timestamp fail
  closed.
- Automatic processing rechecks the current product setting, activation time,
  and event schedule before enqueueing. Event/address/copy sent markers preserve
  one-message idempotency.
- Provider-stage failures without a provider response remain retryable inside
  Resend's deterministic idempotency window instead of becoming terminal.
- The general-purpose Orders audience/backfill controls were removed. English
  and Spanish previews now live in **Settings -> Post-event email**, use unsaved
  settings and a selectable sample event, and never scan or expose recipients.

## Admin and localization contract

- Starts at, Ends at, and Post-event email share one three-column row on desktop
  and stack on narrow screens.
- Related timing/compliance, brand links, support destinations, and support
  amounts use compact setting groups rather than full-width rows.
- Preview preserves the approved Dust Wave English and Spanish email content,
  multipart plain-text/HTML output, visible optional-email opt-out, one-click
  list unsubscribe, postal address, and HTTPS destinations.

## Ethical review

- Automatic email remains limited to one deduplicated confirmed purchaser per
  event, with durable bounce, complaint, opt-out, and sent-marker suppression.
- Activation time and cutoff locking prevent an administrator or an upgraded
  fork from accidentally sending a historical audience after the eligible
  moment.
- The Settings preview renders only sample-event and draft-configuration data;
  customer addresses and order details are absent.
- No attendee names, registration answers, order tokens, ticket links, or
  private fulfillment data enter the email.
