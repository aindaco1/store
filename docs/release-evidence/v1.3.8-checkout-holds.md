## Changes and review

Finite tickets are reserved when Checkout starts. The existing inventory Durable
Object owns deadlines, extensions, creation checkpoints, cancellation and final
confirmation. Required details automatically prepare the Payment Element after
350 ms of settled input. Final Pay and free Complete order remain explicit.
Email is collected once, the optional tip stays at 5%, and the redundant ready
banner and intermediate payment button are removed. See [the lifecycle contract](../CHECKOUT_HOLDS.md).

Release review found that details and navigation remained active while a payment
was being created. The existing busy state now freezes them during preparation;
failure restores editing and an explicit retry. No automatic payment confirmation
or retry loop was added.

Mobile Lighthouse identified oversized portrait-card requests. Preloads and
cards now share their sizing calculation. Quality-80 WebP derivatives for the
two large event posters reduce transfer while preserving the original PNG bytes.
The existing image and asset budgets were retained. The narrowest screenshot
and the compressed artwork were visually reviewed.

## Ethical, security and recovery review

- Money and capacity: the Worker verifies totals and selection; uncertain money
  retains stock and the original payment. Signed webhooks alone confirm paid
  orders. No automatic second charge or manual ambiguous-money recovery path.
- Data and consent: one email entry; reminder consent remains off by default.
  Existing bounded retention, private responses and capability boundaries apply.
  No new analytics, marketing enrollment, admin role or provider was added.
- Access and pressure: the timer represents actual scarce-ticket stock, supports
  extensions, avoids second-by-second announcements, and preserves entered safe
  details after expiry. English/Spanish, keyboard and zoom contracts passed.
- Local synthetic recovery passed. A recent encrypted production snapshot receipt
  was unavailable to the local readiness probe; no captured-data recovery or
  live backup completion is claimed. Physical-device/wallet and VoiceOver speech
  acceptance remain explicit operational follow-ups, not automated-test results.
