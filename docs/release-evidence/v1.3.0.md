## Behavior and audience contract

- New ticket and RSVP drafts enable the follow-up by default. Existing products remain disabled until explicitly enabled.
- A follow-up becomes eligible 24 hours after the configured event end. Delivery uses the durable outbox and deterministic event/address/copy idempotency.
- Confirmed first-party purchasers are deduplicated by normalized email. Imported, launch-test, invalid, suppressed, and already-processed recipients are excluded.
- Email uses the purchaser's checkout language. For one deduplicated address with matching orders in multiple languages, the most recent confirmed checkout language wins.
- Super admins preview the exact email and fresh audience, then must match the audience digest, count, confirmation, and exact acknowledgement before a backfill is queued.
- Promotional opt-out, bounce, and complaint suppression apply only to optional email. Receipts, tickets, security messages, order lookup, and essential fulfillment remain unaffected.

- Sender: `Dust Wave Shop <updates@shop.dustwave.xyz>` with the shared support reply-to.

- Direct support cards are two columns on desktop and stack on narrow screens.

## Ethical review

- The message is a one-time, event-related commercial follow-up with a clear relationship to the purchase, one-message disclosure, physical address, visible opt-out, and separate newsletter consent.
- No attendee names, registration answers, order tokens, ticket links, or private fulfillment data enter the email.
- The audience must be freshly reviewed and cannot be queued from a stale digest. Suppression and idempotency prevent repeated or unwanted optional email.
- Residual operational risk is wrong-event or stale-audience selection; super-admin gating, exact acknowledgement, event eligibility, untruncated scans, and post-queue provider observation mitigate it.
