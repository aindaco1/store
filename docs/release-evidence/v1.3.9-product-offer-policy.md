## Scope and review

Every single or variant Offer now references the existing Organization return
policy by its canonical `@id`. English and Spanish pages share the configured
policy. The default remains no returns; customer-facing terms, checkout prices,
shipping, inventory, payment, fulfillment and storage behavior are unchanged.
The rendered SEO audit rejects missing or mismatched offer references.

The roadmap's checkout capabilities were folded into its existing capability
section to repair two inherited structure-test failures. Physical-device wallet
and VoiceOver acceptance remain future work. Package, config, documentation and
Worker identity versions are synchronized for this patch release.

Ethical review: truthful product policy and public discoverability are the
touched surfaces. No reviews, ratings, shipping charges or delivery promises
were fabricated. No new data collection, provider access or customer messages
are introduced. Residual limitations are tracked below; owner: Store maintainer.
